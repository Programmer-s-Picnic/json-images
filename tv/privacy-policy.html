Privacy Policy — Learn With Champak Browser

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Learn With Champak Browser is a web browser for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. Browser settings and convenience data are primarily stored locally on the device.

Data stored on your device

The app may store bookmarks, visited-link history, open-tab/session information, per-tab privacy settings, quick links, browser preferences, timed-site settings and identifiers for downloaded files. These are used to provide browser features and are stored locally in app preferences unless you choose to clear them.

Web browsing and third-party websites

Web pages are loaded from the sites you choose to visit. Those websites may use cookies, analytics, sign-in systems or other technologies according to their own privacy policies. The app enables normal browser cookies so websites can function. Learn With Champak does not control the data practices of third-party websites.

Google sign-in

Google authentication is opened using Android secure Custom Tabs or an external browser. Google credentials are handled by Google and the browser authentication surface; the app does not ask you to enter or store your Google password.

Downloads

When you choose to download a file, Android's Download Manager saves it to the device. The app may store the identifier of the most recent download so it can offer an Open File action.

Timed site opening

If you configure a timed site, the selected URL and schedule are stored locally. Android alarm and boot-completed capabilities are used only to restore and run that schedule.

Developer data collection

The app does not include a developer-operated account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or custom tracking service in Play Store Release 1. If this changes in a later release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before release.

Clearing data

The app provides controls for clearing visited history, cookies, cache and session data. Android system settings can also clear all app storage.

Children

The browser is a general-purpose tool and is not specifically directed to children. Web content is supplied by the sites a user chooses to visit.
