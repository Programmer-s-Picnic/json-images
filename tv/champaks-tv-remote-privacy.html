Champak's TV Remote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Champak's TV Remote helps you discover, pair with, and control compatible Android TV / Google TV devices on your local network.

Information processed

The app may process local network device identifiers, IP addresses, pairing information and commands you initiate. Pairing certificates and previously entered television-program schedules can be saved on the phone to support reconnection and local use. Text you type to send to the TV is transmitted to the selected TV over the local network.

Network access

The app uses your local network to discover televisions and send remote-control commands to the paired TV. It may use multicast discovery. Pairing communicates with the TV's remote service.

Collection and sharing

The current application does not require an account, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or operate an application backend that collects user profiles. The app does not sell personal data. Local-network data is exchanged with the television you choose to pair with; the TV and its apps may have their own privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

Saved program entries are kept on the phone. Android app data can be cleared or the app uninstalled to delete stored local data. Pairing details may also be cleared by the TV's device-management controls.

Children

The app is a general-purpose TV control utility, not designed to collect information from children.

Changes

This policy will be updated if future versions add online services, telemetry, notifications or other data handling.
