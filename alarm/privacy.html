Champak's Alarm: Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

Champak's Alarm is developed and maintained by Champak Roy. This policy describes the app's alarm features.

Alarm information

Alarm times, labels, repeat days, spoken messages, audio references and preferences are stored in the app's local storage. The app does not provide account registration, advertisements, analytics or a developer-operated cloud synchronization service. Android system backup may include local settings, depending on your device settings.

Audio and speech

You may select an audio file using Android's file picker. The app retains permission to read that selected file for future alarms. It does not request access to your entire media library. Messages are passed to the Android text-to-speech engine installed on your device. That engine may use downloaded voices or its own online services; its privacy practices and connectivity depend on the provider and your settings.

Permissions

Exact alarm access schedules alarms; notifications and full-screen alerts show alarm controls; boot access restores schedules after restart; vibration and a temporary wake lock support ringing. The foreground service plays the alarm until stopped. The app does not request microphone, contacts or location permission.

Deleting information

Delete individual alarms in the app, or clear the app's storage to remove its saved settings. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Support

If you contact support, the information you send is used to respond to your request.
