# Champak's Alarm — store listing draft

App name: Champak's Alarm

Short description: Alarms with spoken reminders, custom sounds and easy testing.

Full description:
Champak's Alarm tells you why your alarm is ringing. Set a wake-up call, a class reminder, or a reminder for your daily routine, then add your own spoken message.

Features:
- Multiple alarms with clear labels.
- One-time alarms or repeats on selected weekdays.
- Choose a phone alarm tune or your own audio file.
- Hear your message after each play of the tune.
- Phone language, English and Hindi speech options, with adjustable speech speed.
- Choose the volume and vibration for each alarm.
- Snooze for your preferred duration.
- Test the full alarm before relying on it.
- See the next alarm and how much time remains.
- Light and dark themes that follow your phone settings.

The app does not require an account and includes no ads. Alarm settings are stored on your device. Android alarm, notification and lock-screen permissions may be needed. Speech availability depends on the text-to-speech engine and voice data installed on your phone. Keep selected audio files available on the device.
